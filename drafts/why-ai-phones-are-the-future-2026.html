What Is an AI Phone? NPU, On-Device AI and Cloud AI

There is no official definition, so here is a practical one: an AI phone has hardware that can run AI models locally, and software that uses it for everyday tasks.

The hardware is the NPU, or neural processing unit. It is a block inside the phone's main chip designed for the maths AI models rely on, and it does that work faster and with less power than the CPU or GPU. Apple calls its version the Neural Engine, Google builds a TPU into its Tensor chips, and Qualcomm and MediaTek put NPUs in their Snapdragon and Dimensity chips. The iPhone 17e's A19, for example, has a 16-core Neural Engine.

Where the work happens matters more than the chip name. On-device AI runs on the phone, works offline and keeps data local. Google's Gemini Nano is one example: it runs inside Android's AICore service without a network connection. Cloud AI sends your request to a server, which allows bigger models but needs a connection. Most AI phones are hybrids. Apple Intelligence handles many tasks on the device and sends heavier ones to Private Cloud Compute, and Google's Private AI Compute does something similar for Gemini features.

One warning for shoppers: the same model name can carry a different chip. The Galaxy S26 and S26+ use Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 in North America, China and Japan and Samsung's Exynos 2600 elsewhere, while the S26 Ultra uses Snapdragon everywhere. AI Phone Features That Are Actually Useful

Camera and photo editing. Scene detection and night mode are years old. The newer tools worth having fix specific problems: object erasers, group-shot fixers such as Samsung's Best Face and Google's Auto Best Take, and Add Me, which puts the photographer into a group photo. The Pixel 10a even adds Camera Coach to guide your framing. Generative edits can look artificial, and many run in the cloud.

Live translation. Apple's Live Translation works across Messages, FaceTime and phone calls, and Samsung's Galaxy AI offers live call translation too. Check the language list and whether the language pack downloads to the phone, because that decides whether it works offline.

Call summaries and transcription. Pixel's Recorder app uses Gemini Nano for on-device summaries, and Samsung's Galaxy A57 and A37 add voice transcription in the recorder. The iPhone 17e adds Call Screening and Hold Assist. These save real time if you handle many calls or meetings.

Assistants. Gemini on Android, Siri AI on the iPhone 18 Pro and the Galaxy S26's mix of Bixby, Galaxy AI and Perplexity all aim to act inside your apps, not just answer questions. Most lean on the cloud, so judge them on what they can actually do. Our ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini comparison helps you choose which assistant to rely on.

Battery. Phones have used small learning models to schedule background activity since Android 9 introduced Adaptive Battery in 2018. The gain is real but modest, and our Android battery life tips cover settings that matter more.

Marketing vs Reality: How to Spot Fake "AI"

Run these five checks before you trust an AI label.

  1. Look for the chip. An honest listing names the processor and says what it does for AI. itel, for instance, highlights its Sola assistant and tools like an AI eraser, but the materials we found give no NPU figures.
  2. Try airplane mode. A feature that still works offline runs on the device. One that stops needs a server. It is the fastest test, and you can do it in a store or during your return window.
  3. Separate presets from AI. "AI camera" often means scene presets that have existed for years. Look for named features such as object removal, group-shot fixing or translation.
  4. Ask whether the assistant is only a shortcut. itel describes the A200's one-tap assistant as powered by the DeepSeek R1 model but does not say whether it runs on the phone or on a server. Ask that question of any budget phone.
  5. Check whether a limit is hardware or marketing. The Pixel 10a runs Camera Coach and Auto Best Take on the older Tensor G4 chip, even though those features were first tied to the newer Tensor G5. Some "exclusive" features can reach older hardware.

Finally, check the update promise. AI features arrive through software updates, so a phone with seven years of updates, like the Pixel 10a, keeps gaining them far longer than one with two.

Are AI Phones Private? What Stays on the Phone

Privacy depends on the feature, not the brand. Features that run on the device, such as Pixel Recorder summaries using Gemini Nano, do not send your request to a server, and Google says Gemini Nano follows Android's Private Compute Core principles. Cloud features send your data to a company's servers.

Hybrid designs try to close the gap. Apple routes heavier requests to Private Cloud Compute, and Google's Private AI Compute processes Gemini requests in a sealed cloud environment that Google says even it cannot access. Those are company claims, so read the details.

Samsung gives you a switch. In Settings, under Galaxy AI, "Process data only on device" keeps Samsung's own features local, at the cost of losing the ones that need more power. It does not cover apps such as Gemini or ChatGPT, which still process in the cloud.

In practice: run the airplane mode test, check which features say they use the cloud, and turn off the ones you do not need. Our guides to privacy-first gadgets and iPhone settings to change now cover the wider setup.

Do You Need a New Phone for AI Features?

Not always. Some Galaxy AI features have reached existing Samsung phones through software updates. Apple Intelligence supports iPhone 15 Pro and Pro Max, and iPhone 16 models or later; the standard iPhone 15 is not supported. Pixel features follow the Tensor generation, though the Pixel 10a shows Google will extend some to older chips. Check your maker's official compatibility list before assuming.

Upgrade if your phone is several years old with no NPU, if updates have ended, or if you want offline features such as on-device translation. Hold off if you mainly want editing tools and your phone still gets updates. Before you spend, see whether your current phone already has features you missed in our guide to hidden Android features.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI phone?

An AI phone is a smartphone with an NPU, a chip block built for AI workloads, and software that uses it for tasks such as translation, photo editing and call transcription. Some of that AI runs on the device and some runs in the cloud.

Which phone has the best AI features?

It depends on your ecosystem. The Galaxy S26 Ultra has the broadest Android feature set, the Pixel 11 pairs Google's newest Tensor G6 chip with Gemini, and the iPhone 18 Pro offers Apple Intelligence with Private Cloud Compute. Pick the platform you already use.

Do I need a new phone for AI?

Not always. Some Galaxy AI features have reached existing Samsung phones through software updates. Apple Intelligence supports iPhone 15 Pro and Pro Max, and iPhone 16 models or later, but not the standard iPhone 15. Check your maker's compatibility list and the upgrade section above.

Are AI phones private?

It depends on the feature. On-device features such as Pixel Recorder summaries stay on the phone, while cloud features send data to servers. Apple and Google add protected cloud systems, and Samsung offers a "Process data only on device" switch. Third-party assistants follow their own policies.

What is the best budget AI phone?

In the US, the Galaxy A37 ($449.99) and Pixel 10a ($499) are the closest options. Elsewhere, the Redmi Note 15 Pro 5G and Infinix Note 60 Pro are strong all-rounders, the Tecno Camon 50 Ultra suits camera fans.

Is AI on phones a gimmick?

Partly. Object removal, translation and call transcription save real time. Scene presets rebranded as AI, and assistants that are only a shortcut to a cloud chatbot, are mostly marketing. The five checks above separate the two.

What is an NPU?

An NPU, or neural processing unit, is a part of a phone's main chip designed to run AI models quickly and efficiently. Apple calls it the Neural Engine, and Google uses a TPU in its Tensor chips. A stronger NPU lets more AI run offline.
